Privacy Policy — EveryRemote

Effective date: October 8, 2026
App: EveryRemote – Universal TV Remote (com.everyremote.app)

EveryRemote turns your phone into a remote control for your smart TV. This policy explains what information the app handles, in plain language. The short version: your data stays on your device, we show no ads, and we sell nothing.

1. Information the app uses

To find and control your TV, EveryRemote needs to see your local Wi-Fi network. Specifically:

  • Local network scanning. The app scans your Wi-Fi network to discover compatible TVs (using standard discovery protocols). This happens on your network only.
  • TV identifiers and names. When you pair a TV, the app saves its network identifier and the name you give it (e.g. "Living Room"), so it can reconnect automatically.
  • App preferences. Settings such as your paired TVs and trial start date are stored on your device.

All of the above is stored only on your phone. It is never uploaded to our servers — we don't operate servers that receive your data.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • No account, no sign-in — we don't know who you are.
  • No location tracking.
  • No contacts, photos, or files.
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads, ever.
  • No analytics SDKs profiling your behavior.

3. Subscriptions and payments

EveryRemote is free to try for 7 days, then requires a $2/month subscription to continue. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or handle your payment card details — Google processes the transaction under its own privacy policy.

4. Crash reporting

To keep the app stable, we may collect anonymous crash and performance diagnostics (for example through Firebase Crashlytics). These reports contain technical information about the crash — such as device model, OS version, and app version — but no personal content and nothing that identifies you.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone, for any reason. The only data that leaves your device is:

  • Anonymous crash diagnostics described above, and
  • Payment processing handled by Google Play (see section 3).

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your TV pairings and preferences live only on your phone, uninstalling the app deletes them. There is nothing stored on our side to delete. If you have a privacy question or request, contact us at the email below and we will respond.

7. Children's privacy

EveryRemote is a general-utility app not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
